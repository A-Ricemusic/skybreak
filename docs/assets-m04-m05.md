# Mission 4 and 5 assets: transport, jammer, tanker, attack helicopter, elite livery

Five new Blender upload assets for missions 4 and 5 (`docs/specs/missions/m04.md`,
`docs/specs/worlds/m04.md`, `docs/specs/missions/m05.md`, `docs/specs/worlds/m05.md`).
They are generated by `tools/assets/m04_m05_assets.py` through the normal
[upload pipeline](roblox-asset-pipeline.md) and the same review pack as the
[mission 1 assets](assets-bomber-carrier.md).

**Status: exported, not uploaded, not wired into the game.** Each folder needs an
independent safety review under the AGENTS.md "Blender asset safety review" gate
(10/10 verdict) before anything is uploaded. The folders are deliberately left out of
`write_manifest.py`'s `KEYS`, so `manifest.json`, `src/shared/UploadedAssetSpecs.ts`
and `src/shared/RobloxAssetIds.ts` do not mention them and nothing in the live game can
load them.

## Regenerate

```sh
tools/assets/build-all.sh heavy-transport radar-jammer fuel-tanker attack-helicopter vanta-v2-elite
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
    --python tools/assets/build.py -- attack-helicopter     # one asset
SKYASSET_DRAFT=1 /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
    --python tools/assets/build.py -- fuel-tanker           # geometry-only Workbench draft in build/draft/
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
    --python tools/assets/verify_fbx.py                     # FBX round trip and hashes for every folder
```

`build-all.sh` ends with `write_manifest.py`, whose output does not change because these
folders are not in `KEYS`. Each build runs `Asset.finish()` (Cycles PBR bake, four standard
previews, FBX with embedded textures, checklist), then `m01_assets.review_pack()`: back,
right-side and bottom orthographic views, more perspective views, close-ups, a
`preview-scale-reference.png` with an already uploaded asset at in-game size (never part of
the FBX), `contact-sheet.png`, the `write_manifest.py` budget checks, and an
`exportVersion` with a SHA-256 for every file except `checklist.json`. The build fails on
any unlisted file in the folder. A rebuild changes the FBX's embedded timestamp and the Eevee
noise, so the reviewer approves the hashes in the committed checklists and the uploader
uploads exactly those files.

## Scale

The fighters are about 1.44 studs per real metre (a 26-stud Vanta for an ~18 m fighter),
the scale the approved heavy bomber and carrier use. Each new asset uses it, so sizes read
correctly next to the Kestrel (22.2 span) and Vanta v2 (24.6 span, 26.4 long). Every
folder has `preview-scale-reference.png`.

## Heavy transport (`heavy-transport`)

Mission 4's three required targets. Original design (D-001): four podded turbofans under
a high wing with 24° leading-edge sweep and 3° anhedral; a cruciform tail with the
tailplanes a third of the way up a 40° swept fin; a flat-sided fuselage with an upswept
tail and a closed cargo ramp drawn as panel lines; flap-track fairings, wingtip fairings and
blade antennas. The gear stows flush, so there are no sponsons. It is unarmed. The
differences from the reference footage's T-tail transport are the cruciform tail, no
winglets, flat sides and no gear sponsons.

Paint is the Vanta enemy family, as on the heavy bomber: `#4a5159` upper and `#6c757e`
lower with the dark splinter camouflage from the wing forward edge aft (none on the nose or
beside the cockpit, where a dark cell read as a side window in the first export). The radome is body colour with
no seam ring or dark panel. The windscreen is a grid of small panes tinted only slightly
lighter than the paint. The only markings are a plain dark-red (`#6e2a24`) fin-top band and
rings behind the engine lips.

## Radar jammer (`radar-jammer`)

Mission 4's two optional jammers. Original design: a low wing with 30° sweep and 5°
dihedral, two podded turbofans, a T-tail with a bullet fairing, and a 14.4-stud lens
rotodome on a single swept pylon aft of the wing. It also has wingtip antenna pods, a
belly fairing and blade antennas. The rotodome is static and part of `Body`; its centre is
in the checklist (`rotodome`) if the game wants to add a spinning effect later. The
reference's jammer is a twin airliner with a strut-mounted rotodome. This one differs in
the T-tail, the single pylon and the wingtip pods. It is unarmed. Paint matches the
transport, with plain rib circles on the rotodome faces.

## Fuel tanker (`fuel-tanker`)

Mission 5's two ships at sea. Original medium tanker: a raked stem with a bulbous bow, a
parallel midbody and a transom stern. It has a raised forecastle with a breakwater and the
deckhouse aft (three tiers plus a bridge with wings), a raked funnel and an engine casing.
On deck are three cargo lines on racks, a two-pipe manifold midships with valves and drip
trays, a raised catwalk, tank hatches, a hose crane, a foremast, mooring winches (aft and
midships only), a free-fall lifeboat on a stern ramp and a rescue boat on a davit.

- **Orientation:** bow at Roblox -Z. The hose crane, the catwalk (just starboard of the
  pipes) and the rescue boat are on starboard = Roblox +X. The plan is authored with
  starboard +x and mirrored to Blender -X, as the carrier is.
  `preview-top-orientation.png` adds a review-only arrow from stern to bow (not in the FBX).
- **Waterline:** `groundOffsetStuds` 16: the waterline is 16 studs above the keel (the
  lowest point). Place the bottom-centre pivot 16 studs below the sea surface. Main deck
  26 studs above the keel.
- **Paint:** charcoal topsides, a black boot-top, red antifouling, a green-grey deck, an
  off-white deckhouse with rows of plain windows, a plain funnel with a soot-dark top. There
  is no hull number, name, draught mark or funnel emblem.
- No hawse pipes or anchors at the bow, and no winches on the forecastle. Head-on they
  would show as a symmetric pair of dark marks above the stem.

The legacy frigate (430 studs) is authored at 0.28 m per stud and is oversized next to
this tanker. Keep them apart or scale the frigate down.

## Attack helicopter (`attack-helicopter`)

Mission 5's three rig defenders. Original tandem-seat design: a narrow faceted fuselage
and a stepped two-pane canopy (gunner in front and low, pilot behind and high). Both
engines sit under one wide faired engine deck with side intake grilles, and the exhausts
point sideways from its rear corners, so from behind they are short tubes rather than two
dark round openings. Stub wings carry two small empty pylons and a tip pod each. The tail has a swept fin, a low stabiliser and a tail skid. There is no gun, chin
turret, sensor ball, rockets or missiles.

- **Parts:** `Body`, `MainRotor` (four blades, hub and grips) and `TailRotor` (four
  blades, on the left/port side, Roblox -X). Each rotor part has its hub as the part
  origin. The checklist's `rotors` gives the hub in Roblox model space, the spin axis and
  the radius, so the game can spin them. This is the only reason the rotors are separate
  parts.
- **Canopy:** opaque panes tinted only slightly lighter and bluer than the paint (`#57636c`),
  with body-colour frames at each bow and at the sills. There is no dark glazing and no crew.
- **Changed after the first full export (before commit):** the exhausts first pointed aft,
  so the back view showed two dark round openings above the boom; they now point sideways.
  A roof centre-line frame crossed the bows in plus-shaped marks; it was removed.
- **Face check, changed during drafting:** the first draft had two separate engine nacelles
  with rounded fronts beside the mast. Head-on they read as two round shapes above the
  canopy, so they were replaced by one wide engine deck before any export.

## Elite squadron livery (`vanta-v2-elite`)

Mission 5's five-ship elite flight. It uses the uploaded Vanta v2 mesh with a new paint:
blue-black countershading (`#1d222b` upper, `#2b313b` lower), darker splinter camouflage
(`#151920`, `#262c36`) and a `#14171c` radome. The whole fin and rudder are pale steel
blue (`#7f97a8`), and one straight chordwise band crosses each outer wing. There is no
emblem, chevron, pair of stripes, numeral-like mark or text. The first export used a pale
fin-tip block on a dark fin. In the side view it met the pale rudder-hinge gap (the
background shows through the Vanta v2's fin/rudder gap, which stands out on dark paint) in
a shape like the numeral "7". The fin is now one pale field, so the gap cannot draw a line on
it. It is distinct from Vulture (dark grey
with a bone feather band) and Rusk (oxblood with a gold pinstripe).

`aircraft_v2.vanta_v2()` was split into `vanta_v2_airframe()`, which builds the mesh and
zones, and the `vanta-v2` builder, which finishes it with the Vulture and Rusk liveries.
The elite builder calls the same `vanta_v2_airframe()` with its own name, paint and export
version. The folder is therefore a complete asset (mesh, all four maps, previews) with the
same geometry, parts, hinges and attachments as `vanta-v2`. The uploaded `vanta-v2`,
`vanta-v2-vulture` and `vanta-v2-rusk` folders were not rebuilt and are unchanged. The elite's normal map is byte-identical to
`vanta-v2-normal.png` (same SHA-256), which confirms the same mesh and UVs. Its metalness
map differs because the elite paint uses metalness 0.08 instead of 0.06.

## Concerns for the safety reviewer and the uploader

- **Faces:** check `preview-front`, `preview-front-low`, `preview-front-high` and the
  `closeup-front*` views of the transport, jammer and helicopter, plus `preview-front` and
  `preview-closeup-bow` of the tanker. The creator's own notes on what was checked are in
  each checklist's `noFaces` note.
- **Glyph-like marks:** look at the elite's fin in `preview-side` and `preview-closeup-fin`.
  The Vanta v2's fin/rudder gap still shows as a faint line on the pale fin; it is the same
  geometry as the uploaded `vanta-v2`. On the tanker's `preview-top`, the bridge-roof mast
  is now a single short bar (its two radar bars were first offset and drew parallel
  strokes). The windscreens of the transport and jammer are fine pane grids (`closeup-front*`).
- **Weapons:** only the elite livery shows the Vanta's existing pylon missiles, which were
  already approved on `vanta-v2`. Every other asset is unarmed. The helicopter's pylons are
  empty.
- **Orientation:** the tanker and helicopter are asymmetric. They use
  `m01_assets.roblox_model` (Blender `(x, y, z)` to Roblox `(-x, z, y)`) for their records.
  As with the carrier, no asymmetric upload has been checked in Studio yet. Check the bow
  direction and the starboard features after import.
- **Texture resolution:** the tanker uses 512² maps over 260 studs, so the deckhouse windows
  and deck fittings are soft up close (the same limit as the frigate and carrier).

## Wiring after approval (not done here)

1. Upload each approved `<asset>.fbx` (the hashes in its checklist) as a Model asset.
2. Add the folders to `write_manifest.KEYS`, for example `"heavy-transport": "HeavyTransport"`,
   `"radar-jammer": "RadarJammer"`, `"fuel-tanker": "FuelTanker"`,
   `"attack-helicopter": "AttackHelicopter"` and `"vanta-v2-elite": "VantaV2Elite"`. Add
   `vanta-v2-elite` to `PROCEDURAL` and `V2_AIRCRAFT` like the other Vanta liveries. The
   other aircraft are authored at game size and keep gameScale 1; `texture_limit` must
   allow 1024 for them, as it does for the heavy bomber. Rerun `write_manifest.py`, then
   add the IDs to `RobloxAssetIds.ts`.
3. The helicopter needs `MainRotor` and `TailRotor` allowed as parts by the loader and
   spun about their hubs. The tanker goes through `WorldAssets.PlaceUploaded` at its
   waterline.

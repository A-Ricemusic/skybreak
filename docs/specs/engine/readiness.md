### Crossing-time calculation and clock boundaries

At **160 studs/s cruise = 800 displayed mph**, five minutes of continuous straight flight covers **48,000 studs**; at **600 studs/s max = 3,000 displayed mph**, **180,000 studs**. The ledger's 4:54 segment yields 47,040 / 176,400 studs respectively. The current hard-boundary square is 7,600 studs wide: **47.5 s cruise / 12.67 s max** edge-to-edge; the soft width 7,200 gives 45 / 12 seconds. The ±2400 authored objective envelope is only 30 s across at cruise. These are calculations from `src/shared/FlightMath.ts:91`–`:100`, `:220`–`:229` and `src/shared/WorldAirspace.ts:4`, not measurements of reference map dimensions.

The ledger places carrier intro at 0:06:30–~0:06:59, quote at ~0:07:00–0:07:08, control around 0:07:10, accomplishment around 0:11:04 and tail to 0:11:24. Thus 4:54 includes intro/tail; roughly 234 seconds is control-to-accomplishment, with the ledger's ~±5-second event uncertainty. Do not lengthen combat to 4:54 plus intro accidentally. Under D-016 derive route distances as `160 × desired cruise transit seconds`; budget engagement turns and acquisition separately. A 48,000-wide map would be 6.32× today's hard width and roughly 40× its area, not a default requirement. Keep weapon ranges and flight tuning intact; adapt encounter spacing rather than stretching combat ranges with the scenery.

Save-side deadline checks also need updating: `src/server/CampaignRepository.ts:445`, `:469` and `:575` currently qualify wins and timeouts against the global limit; changing only the runner would reject valid long sorties.

World configuration must travel to client prediction before control handoff; editing `WorldAirspace` globals alone changes every current mission. Planner extent/far tiles (`src/shared/WorldPlan.ts:71`), objective validation (`src/shared/MissionCatalog.ts:229`), spawn clamping and radar must agree. An audited bounded coastal combat area plus sparse sea horizon is the recommended first slice; the detailed reference spec must determine its transit distances.

## 5. Risks, limits and decision handoff

1. **World scaling is not a bounds constant change.** Old terrain extent is ±4000 with flat far tiles to ±6000 (`src/shared/WorldPlan.ts:71`, `src/server/Scenery.ts:285`). Cruise traversal can already cross the hard square six times during five minutes. Enlarging a full sea slab or ridge ring multiplies voxel work and replication; use timed route distances and sparse ocean composition rather than scaling every prop/weapon. Current presets/fog do not establish a reference-sized horizon. Biggest unresolved M1 design input: desired first-contact/crossing time and route extents from the detailed spec.
2. **Terrain scheduling bounds jobs, not total load time or worst single-call stall.** `src/shared/TerrainSchedule.ts:16`–`:22` caps fills at 262,144 cells, bump slices at 32,768, unsplit regions at 4,194,304 and budget at 12 ms. `:123` checks budget after work; a long individual Roblox call cannot be preempted. `src/server/TerrainWriter.ts:64` executes those jobs. `docs/world-realism.md:103` reports old M5 **96.2M voxel writes / 2.20M hill cells**, close to 100M / 2.5M budget; `:106` M6 reaches 2.36M hill cells. `:72` records an unsplit ~3.0M-voxel ridge ball. Budgeted build telemetry (`:77`) must be measured again for M1 in engine; no measured build seconds or mobile FPS are supplied by those tables.
3. **Streaming/mobile and instance cost.** `default.project.json:38` and `test.project.json:38` disable streaming. `docs/world-realism.md:117` reports old M19 **617 estimated instances, 117 plan parts, 168 props, 14 unique meshes**, versus sparse old M23's **30 instances, 22 parts, 4 props** (`:121`). Planner budgets are 340 parts / 280 props / 20 meshes / 700 terrain ops (`src/shared/WorldPlan.ts:75`); `Scenery` separately warns above 600 primitive parts (`src/server/Scenery.ts:20`, `:97`), not a total-memory guarantee. `countPlan` estimates props as MeshPart + SurfaceAppearance (`src/shared/WorldPlan.ts:1245`); a complex carrier/bomber needs real descendant/triangle/texture accounting. Phones already disable effects and cap clouds/weather (`docs/world-realism.md:86`); do not spend that headroom speculatively. Enabling streaming is a separate lifecycle/target replication change, not a free scale fix.

Additional constraints:

- World lifecycle is single-player and clears global Terrain (`src/server/MissionWorld.ts:12`, `:218`); `WorldBuilder` is the menu hangar builder (`src/server/WorldBuilder.ts:238`), not the mission generator. Keep carrier/intro state inside the existing disposable sortie and restore the menu on exit. `src/server/RelayWorld.ts:1`–`:22` documents executor ordering, preload deadline and fallbacks; source plan audits do not validate assembled rendered appearance.
- Bomber/escort sequencing must fit the queue. Existing AirRequest only allows bandit/raider (`src/shared/WavePlan.ts:23`), while courier/ace are spawned separately. Add new roles without bypassing the shared budget or spawning mandatory bombers behind an unkillable optional queue. The seven-aircraft cap may require scheduling compromises; do not silently raise it to match a later large air battle.
- Asset provenance/review can block visual readiness. Current assets have no bomber/carrier and D-006 provisionally preserves the existing two aircraft. Decide whether distinct procedural non-player target geometry is within that default; recommend yes, preserving player/escort models. If Blender is used, independent review must inspect exact exports/textures and assembled materials/UVs, consult current official Roblox policies and record hashes/previews/reviewer/date with explicit 10/10 safety approval before any upload. This research uploads nothing and makes no moderation guarantee.
- Missing exact reference values block **calibrated fidelity**, not foundational engine PRs. The ledger does not establish rank thresholds, all target values, all arrival triggers, exact countdown or geometry. The detailed spec must close those gaps before claiming M1 matches; sparse samples and an automated pilot cannot establish owner acceptance.

### Enclosed flight and duel acceptance

Proposal: each groove is 180 studs wide ×140 studs clear high, with about 2,880 studs from mouth to generator; these are deliberately explicit blockout assumptions pending flight tests, not reference measurements. At 160 studs/s the straight run lasts 18 s; turns must fit the unchanged player turn envelope. Use simplified convex collision with no invisible beam protrusions, roof occlusion for external weapons, internal lock visibility and safe warning margins. The central route needs a visible escape aperture and the 30:00 updated phase that remains achievable without collision exploits. Test entry from all permitted headings, missed shots, overshoot, retry/reset and a core kill without exit (must not win).

After the exit, disable every structure gun and clear existing hostile projectiles, remove all surviving enemy fighters, order allies to hold fire and stage Rusk above the smoking structure without a loading screen. The original duel arena is open air, not a trench. Keep the broken works visible underneath, preserve player damage, rack count, special charges and missile-regeneration progress (8-missile rack, one missile every 2.5 s; no transition refill or finite reserve) and keep Rusk inside bounds. Only Rusk’s destruction advances to the finale; destroying the core cannot emit mission victory. The storm treatment persists into the duel; the finale may move to the city after the storm according to the chapter map.

## 5. Lighting and atmosphere

Clear arid daylight with a pale-blue sky and long-range haze; scene is not the old Broken Relay night relay. Emplacement shadows and moving barrels establish scale. Native colour values are not calibrated material albedo. Keep central core visible on low passes while radar is jammed. Elite arrival needs a short original formation reveal/camera and safe return to control, preserving a paused mission clock.

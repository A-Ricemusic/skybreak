## 2. Layout and scale

### HUD units and conversion

SPEED interpreted as km/h, range/ALT as feet following accepted M02–M05 conventions; units/datum are inferred, not printed unit labels. Conversion: reference metres×**0.96 studs/m**; feet×0.3048×0.96≈0.292608 studs. Add the chosen world datum to absolute heights. Vertical datum/terrain-relative readings remain uncertain. Use flight travel times to calibrate scale before final geometry (D-015/D-016), with existing 160 studs/s cruise as the comparison baseline.

## 5. Lighting and atmosphere

Use broad diffuse daylight, pale yellow-grey horizon and grey-white high cloud. Rock faces remain readable in near terrain flight; snow is not self-illuminated. Do not reuse M03’s deep clear-blue sky unchanged. No night lights/searchlights/rain. Readable friendly labels and altitude-band warnings matter more than expensive volumetric effects. Briefing CONDITION at 2:38:55 reads 55%cloud at 15,000 ft, altostratus; a literal conversion puts the base at 4,421 studs (+32), above the existing hard ceiling. This is additional evidence for reviewing vertical/cloud compression together. Sun azimuth is unknown.

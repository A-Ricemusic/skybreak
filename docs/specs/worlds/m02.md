# World spec: mission 2 (reference "Imminent Threat")

Companion to the [mission 2 spec](../missions/m02.md); see that file for the clip, the timestamp mapping and coverage. All times are source h:mm:ss in the GamePlayShare upload (ledger: [reference/ledger.md](../../../reference/ledger.md)). Places are described generically (D-001). The world is built procedurally, like today's world builder (D-012), at full time-matched scale with level of detail (D-019, D-028).

Evidence for this file: 417 gameplay frames at 1 fps on contact sheets, full-resolution frames of the briefing map (0:14:45), the select screen (0:14:58–0:15:02), the opening lock (0:16:06) and an overhead base view (0:18:17), and native SPEED/ALT crops every 4 s from 0:15:42 to 0:17:54. Colours are 1×1 area averages of full-resolution frames: **screen colours after the game's haze and grading, not material albedo**. Positions on the base are read by eye from oblique views and are low confidence unless stated.

## 2. Layout and scale

### HUD units

As in mission 1 (world spec m01 §2): **SPEED in km/h, ALT and target range in feet**. Mission 2 adds a check from AWACS range calls: the base is called at 8 miles (0:17:15–0:17:21) and 4 miles (0:17:30–0:17:35). Closing 4 statute miles = 6.4 km in 15 s is 430 m/s, against HUD SPEED 1,300–1,570 (361–436 m/s if km/h). Consistent with km/h. (Knots would give 670–810 m/s; rejected.)

ALT over the runway on the strafing pass reads 54–69 (0:18:23–0:18:25), so the base sits close to ALT zero. Whether ALT is above sea level or above ground is not provable here (see m03 world §2).

### Scale factor

**k = 0.96 studs per reference metre** (D-022), unchanged from mission 1. The reference cruise is the same aircraft class at a similar normal-thrust SPEED (480–650 at 0:15:38–0:15:54, against mission 1's ~600). 960 studs per km.

**Fit with the mission 1 world size.** At k = 0.96 the start-to-base distance is 25,900–30,700 studs, and the playable area must extend beyond the base for the fight around it (a few km). Mission 1's world is 32,768 studs square, flyable to a soft edge of 14,000 from the centre (D-033). **Recommendation:** place the base centre about **25,000 studs** north of the start (just under the lower estimate, about a 4% compression), with the start at z = +12,500 and the base centre at z = −12,500 (north is −Z, as in mission 1), so the base and its north edge stay inside the soft edge. This is a recommendation for the orchestrator; it is not a reading. The alternative is a larger world.

- Start: (0, 0), heading 360° (HUD N at 0:15:38), ALT 1,600 ft = 488 m. At k = 0.96 that is 468 studs above the ground datum.
- Substation: (0, ~9.7), directly under the power line. Locked at 6,532 ft range from 1,500 ft at 0:16:06, so first visible about 2 km ahead.
- Power line: lattice pylons with red and white bands run from the start area north to the base (AWACS navigation cue, 0:15:39–0:15:43; pylons visible 0:16:15–0:16:23; a red-white mast beside the base at 0:17:40–0:17:41).
- Base centre: about (−4, 28) ± 3 km. Its bearing from the player swung from 340° to 320° during the run-in (0:17:15 → 0:17:30), so the base lies a little west of the power line's line of approach.
- Lake: along the west side of the base. Seen on the player's left while heading roughly north (0:18:38–0:18:42) and filling the horizon behind the base from the west (0:21:16–0:21:41). Fuel tanks and buildings stand on its shore at the base's south-west corner (0:19:32, 0:20:03, 0:21:57). Which side is not fully certain: the player's heading is not always readable.
- Wooded valley and ridges: west and north-west of the base, below the lake end (0:20:14–0:20:35, 0:20:44–0:21:10).
- Mission boundary and return line: the briefing's dashed line passes through the friendly marker due south of the base (0:14:45). Its meaning is a return line (m03 world §2); no return was flown here.

## 5. Lighting and atmosphere

- **Key light:** flat overcast at 13:29, no sun disc, soft shadowless lighting. Diagonal light shafts appear in some frames.
- **Haze:** moderate grey-green; terrain detail fades by a few km.
- **Cloud:** a full altostratus deck well above the action (8,000 ft briefing) plus low mist at 1,500–2,000 ft early on. No cloud below the player over the base.
- **Grade:** desaturated green-grey; warm colour only in explosions and napalm fire.
- **Fire and smoke:** burning hangars and aircraft leave black smoke columns over the base for the rest of the mission (0:21:57–0:22:30). Cosmetic.

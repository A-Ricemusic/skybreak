# World spec: mission 3 (reference "The Northern Eye")

Companion to the [mission 3 spec](../missions/m03.md); see that file for the clip, the timestamp mapping and coverage. All times are source h:mm:ss in the GamePlayShare upload (ledger: [reference/ledger.md](../../../reference/ledger.md)). Places are described generically (D-001). The world is built procedurally (D-012) at full time-matched scale with level of detail (D-019, D-028).

Evidence for this file: 463 frames at 1 fps on contact sheets (0:26:41–0:34:23), the tactical briefing map cropped at full resolution (0:25:44), the select screen (0:25:56), SPEED/ALT crops every 3–4 s for the ingress, the site 2 run and the return, the landing DIST counter, and four-frame full-resolution composites of the sites and the landing. Colours are 1×1 area averages of full-resolution frames, **screen colours after haze and grading, not albedo**; the snow figure mixes lit and shaded snow.

## 2. Layout and scale

### HUD units

SPEED in km/h and range in feet, as in missions 1 and 2. AWACS range calls give one check: site 1 at 4 miles at 0:28:20–0:28:25, first bomb on site 1 at 0:28:43, 23 s later at SPEED ~1,000–1,260 (0:28:17–0:28:23), which is 6.4–8.1 km: consistent with statute miles and km/h.

**ALT is probably height above ground, not above sea level** (inference). The player flew valley floors and gorges at ALT 1,250–1,400 with rock walls rising well above the jet (0:26:41–0:27:40, 0:29:22–0:29:27), and the landing ALT fell to about 0 at touchdown. With ALT measured above sea level the mountains could be only a few hundred feet tall, which the frames contradict. This also means ALT gives no summit heights.

**Landing DIST counter.** DIST fell 4,132 → 311 in 29 s (0:33:35 → 0:34:04), 132 units/s at SPEED 316–360 (88–100 m/s). The controller calls "2 miles" at DIST ≈ 3,300 and "1 mile" at DIST ≈ 1,700–1,900, which fits metres or yards; neither fits the speed exactly. The unit is unknown; the landing is a cinematic and needs no exact match.

### Scale factor

**k = 0.96 studs per reference metre** (D-022), unchanged. Normal-thrust SPEED is similar to missions 1 and 2 (480–720 at 0:26:41–0:27:01).

## 5. Lighting and atmosphere

- **Key light:** strong, clear afternoon sun at a moderate height; hard shadows on the snow with blue shadow tones; strong glare and streaks into the sun.
- **Sky:** deep blue zenith, pale haze band at the horizon, no cloud. A small moon is visible.
- **Snow look:** bright but not blown out; blue-grey in shade.
- **Landing scene:** dimmer, bluish, with a low sun near the horizon. Treat it as a separate lighting state, or keep the mission lighting for simplicity (owner choice).
- **Smoke:** grey-white smoke columns rise from destroyed sites (0:29:11–0:29:13).

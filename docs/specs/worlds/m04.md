## 2. Layout and scale

### Scale factor

Same game, aircraft class and HUD as mission 1, so the D-022 factor applies: **k = 0.96 studs per reference metre** (reference cruise about 600 km/h against SKYBREAK's 160 studs/s). This mission's normal-thrust readings (486 at the start, 600–950 in the hunt) agree with that cruise range.

**Fit.** A straight transport route lasting the footage's 5:40 at the measured speed would be about 42,000–53,000 studs long, longer than mission 1's 32,768-stud world. The enlarged in-flight map keeps the fight near its centre (0:38:16, 0:41:08), so the transports probably did not fly far in a straight line. Options (for the orchestrator; not decided here):

1. **Recommended:** a 32,768-stud world (as mission 1, D-033) with a curving route: north-east from the start area, then a long hook east and south-east toward an exit at the world edge "toward the harbour". About 40,000–45,000 studs at ~140 studs/s gives 4.8–5.4 minutes, close to the footage's 5:40 to the last kill.
2. Slow the transports (D-035 style) to fit a straight 25,000-stud route. This loses the measured closing feel (the player closed at only 55–74 m/s on normal thrust).

## 5. Lighting and atmosphere

- **Key light:** high midday sun (12:26); azimuth unread. Hard but hazy light on terrain.
- **Haze:** strong cool-blue aerial perspective from altitude; terrain loses detail within a few km.
- **Clouds:** sparse cumulus near 5,500 ft (1,610 studs + 32 at k = 0.96), at the top of the fight band, plus thin high wisps.
- **Colour grade:** saturated blue sky, green-grey ground; warmer than mission 1.

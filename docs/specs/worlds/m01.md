## 2. Layout and scale

### HUD units (needed for any distance)

The HUD never shows units. The working hypothesis is **SPEED in km/h, ALT and target range in feet**:

- **Climb check.** From 0:09:51 to 0:09:52 ALT rises 3,420 → 3,579 (+159/s) at SPEED 415–430. If SPEED is km/h (415 km/h = 115 m/s), ALT cannot be metres, because 159 m/s exceeds 115 m/s. In feet, 159 ft/s = 48 m/s, which is a 25° climb: plausible.
- **Cloud check.** The briefing gives the cloud base at 6,000 ft, and the player flies under cloud at ALT 2,000, which fits feet.
- **Tail-chase check** (mission spec §6):
  - 0:07:50–0:07:55: closing 430 ft/s = 131 m/s at 1,430 km/h = 397 m/s, so the bomber flies at 397 − 131 = 266 m/s.
  - 0:10:28–0:10:33: closing 708 ft/s = 216 m/s at 1,400 km/h = 389 m/s, so the bomber flies at 173 m/s.

  Both are believable for a turboprop bomber. With range in metres the first case gives a negative bomber speed, so metres is rejected.

### Scale factor (D-016)

- Reference cruise: HUD SPEED about **600** on normal thrust (597–659 at 0:07:18–0:07:36) = 600 / 3.6 = **166.7 m/s**.
- SKYBREAK cruise: `FlightMath.CruiseSpeed` = **160 studs/s**.
- **k = 160 / 166.7 = 0.96 studs per reference metre** (960 studs per km, or 0.293 studs per HUD foot). A distance in km becomes studs by multiplying by 960.
- Caveat: top speeds do not scale the same way. The reference player peaks at about 1,660 km/h, 2.8× cruise (0:09:36). SKYBREAK's `MaxSpeed` of 600 is 3.75× cruise. So at full throttle a SKYBREAK pilot crosses this world about 1.3× faster than the reference player did.
- Bomber speed at this scale: 173–266 m/s × 0.96 = **166–255 studs/s**, roughly 1.0–1.6× our cruise. Recommended: about 200 studs/s, so bombers can only be caught above cruise, as in the reference.

**Discrepancy.** If the airbase is on the island, at most ~10 km from its south coast (map estimate), the bombers would need only ~40–60 s to reach it at the measured speed, not 154 s. So the bombers either fly a longer route (the city first, then the airbase, maybe not straight), or the island is about 3× larger than the map suggests. Builders should **match times**: about 1 minute from control to first kill, ~67 s to the city strike call, ~188 s to the airbase drop. Route length then follows from the bomber speed chosen.

**Fit with the current airspace.** At k = 0.96 the playable region measures at least ~6 km (start to coast) plus a ~10 km island, which is ≥ 16 km N–S: ≥ 15,400 studs, and up to ~35,000 studs for the bomber route. Today's world is flyable to ±3,600 studs (`WorldAirspace.soft`), detailed to ±4,000 and flat out to ±6,000. **The D-016 world is about 2–5× wider than today's airspace.** This needs an orchestrator or owner decision; options are listed in §6.

## 5. Lighting and atmosphere

- **Key light:** diffuse overcast with a high sun (14:05). Azimuth unreadable: the sun disc appears without a heading reference at 0:08:32, 0:09:53 and 0:10:02. Soft shadows; low contrast on terrain.
- **Haze:** strong, cool blue-grey aerial perspective. Terrain and sea converge to about RGB(120,145,180) toward the horizon. Ground detail goes flat and blue within a few km.
- **Cloud layers:**
  - A thin overcast deck above the player's 2,000 ft.
  - Broken cumulus around the briefing's 6,000 ft. The player climbs into wisps at ALT ~3,000–4,600 (0:09:46–0:10:07).
  - No low cloud below 2,000 ft over the island.
- **Light shafts:** large, soft, diagonal crepuscular rays in many shots. A signature look of this mission.
- **Colour grade:** desaturated cool blue-teal overall. Warm colour only in explosions and the afterburner.
- **Fog:** none beyond the haze.

**Options for the world-extent decision** (for the orchestrator and owner; not decided here):

1. **Enlarge the airspace for this mission** to about ±10,000 studs, with LOD terrain (coarse voxels or a mesh far from the paths) and props only near the bomber route. This is the closest to D-016. It needs engine work on `WorldAirspace`, `WorldPlan.EXTENT`, the budgets and the radar range.
2. **Keep ±3,600 and compress.** Shrink the island to fit and slow the bombers or lengthen their route with a dogleg so the time stays right (about 1 min to first kill, ~3 min to the airbase drop). This breaks D-016's literal geometry but keeps its intent (time).
3. **Hybrid:** play in ±3,600 around the bomber route, with a scaled-down far island silhouette as backdrop.

## 5. Lighting and atmosphere

Native briefing 3:07:35 reads 100%stratus at 3000 ft. Use diffuse grey daylight and volumetric-looking depth through cheap fog/haze layers; near rock and balloon hull must stay readable for guns-only visual targeting. The map explicitly warns radar failure and visual gun use (3:07:35), so fog must obscure navigation without making mandatory point targets invisible at firing distance. Do not introduce lightning/rain merely because the chapter mood is “storm.” Original radio describes visibility and guidance; audio was not evaluated.

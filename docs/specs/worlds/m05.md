## 2. Layout and scale

**Scale from the AWACS distance calls.** At 0:45:10 (25 s after the start, heading north at about 770 km/h = 214 m/s, so about 5.3 km north) AWACS puts the rig at bearing 050, 8 miles. If the player started at the base and "miles" are nautical (14.8 km) or statute (12.9 km), the rig is 16.9–18.7 km from the base at bearing 036–037°. That agrees with the map bearing (031°) and gives **about 100 m per map pixel**: a map square of about 72 km. The second call, refinery at bearing 290, 8 miles (0:53:12), came after an unknown autopilot and dogfight path, so it is not used for scale.

## 4. Landmarks and structures

### Offshore rig complex (east zone)

Seen at 0:46:41–0:50:27 from ALT 250–1,100 ft (frames 0:47:27, 0:47:46):

- Several lattice-steel platforms on legs, each topped with lattice **cranes** and **towers**, joined by long straight **trestle walkways and pipelines** carried on rows of legs (a chain of 6–8 target boxes along one trestle at 0:47:46).
- Estimated extent: a line of linked platforms about 1.5–3 km long (eyeball, low confidence) → 1,400–2,900 studs.
- Targets (mission spec §4): cranes 50, towers 60, control rooms 60, SAM 30–50, AA guns 20. Tanker 2 is nearby. Helicopters (3) defend it.

### Land refinery (west zone)

Seen at 0:54:14–0:55:57 from ALT 200–900 ft (frames 0:54:27, 0:55:41):

- A dense grid of round **fuel tanks** (two sizes, 40 and 50 points) and smaller **gas tanks** (30) on the coastal plain.
- **Refinery units** (60), a very tall **smokestack** (60; an ally remarks on the huge chimney at 0:55:08), **pump stations**, **control rooms** (60) and **AA guns** (20).
- Thousands of small white **point lights** across the complex, and **searchlight beams** sweeping the sky (0:54:27–0:55:10).
- Estimated extent: about 2 × 3 km (low confidence) → about 1,900 × 2,900 studs.

## 5. Lighting and atmosphere

- **Ambient:** low, cool blue. The moon is a small disk high in the sky; faint stars.
- **Local light:** refinery point lights (white, dense), searchlight cones (white, sweeping), explosion and fire glow (orange) as targets burn. Tanks burn with large fireballs.
- **Glare:** a very bright low light with radiating rays at times (above).
- **Haze:** thin; the horizon fades to dark blue.
- **Cloud:** sparse cirrus near 14,000 ft (sky dressing, above the 3,600-stud flight ceiling).

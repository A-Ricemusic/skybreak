## 2. Summary

Attack a giant circular gun installation in an arid crater. Eight physical emplacements form the landmark, but only seven are live required gun targets. A central jammer makes the first attack visually guided; destroying it restores radar. Clear the guns while avoiding their fire, with optional air defences and aircraft and a mid-mission resupply. After the last gun falls an elite formation arrives; one elite kill ends this run as the others withdraw. In Broken Relay that fallen pilot is Vulture Two, whose ejection must be preserved for the confession.

- Individual elite identities and why one loss triggers retreat; adaptation selects Vulture Two intentionally.
- Whether jammer is logically required or only practically helpful. Keep it enabling/optional until proven otherwise.
- Gun projectile collision/damage and shielding rules; no failed run. Proposed failure: player lost/crash/out-of-bounds; strike timer expires with live guns; elite clock expires without defeating Vulture Two.

## 2. Summary

Protect two civilian airliners at widely separated altitude bands over snowy mountains in bright overcast. Hostile fighters attack the airliners rather than simply chasing the player. One airliner cannot climb, forcing repeated high/low transitions. The successful run destroys eleven fighters, including a named ace, and finishes with 840/S in 5:13.28 of objective time.

## 2. Summary

A low approach over dark water to three defended beaches below steep coastal hills. The player suppresses gun emplacements, armour and AA while landing troops report their progress. Helicopters contest the shore. A carrier-service detour replenishes weapons. At the ten-minute phase expiry, attack jets close on the landing and become the new priority; destroying the final attack aircraft ends the sortie before the second clock expires.

Observed final result: **4,490 points / rank S** (2:14:25). This is a demonstrated S result, **not an exact S cutoff**. See §7 before choosing rank cuts.

## 2. Summary

A daytime air intercept high above forested hills, under a clear blue sky. The player starts airborne at 4,000 ft, heading roughly NNE, with friendly fighters. Three large enemy transports, escorted by fighters, are flying supplies toward an enemy harbour. Two airborne radar jammers nearby blank the player's radar, so the transports must first be found by eye. AWACS sends the player to destroy the jammers to restore radar. After the first jammer falls, AWACS reports a second one still active (a mid-mission twist). The three transports are the only required targets. The jammers and roughly a dozen escorts (fighters of three classes, including one named ace) are optional but score points. More escorts arrive than the briefing lists. The mission ends the moment the third transport dies. There is no RTB and no landing. A 12:00 countdown runs on the HUD. The footage player finished with 6:19.38 left (5:40.61 elapsed), scoring 1,540 points and rank S.

## 10. Engine gaps (today's SKYBREAK)

From reading `src/shared/missions/m01.ts`, `MissionCatalog.ts`, `MissionPolicy.ts`, `BomberEncounter` use in m01, `WeaponRules.ts` and `SpecialWeapons.ts` at `main` 07526a2. Engineers should confirm.

1. **Radar jamming tied to live jammers.** Nothing today blanks the radar or hides contacts. Needed: a jam state (radar noise, no blips, maybe no lock beyond visual range) that clears in steps as each jammer dies.
2. **Optional jammer aircraft with a twist call.** The first jammer's death triggers "another jammer active". `PhaseTwist` can fire a radio line on progress, but the jammers are optional, not phase progress.
3. **Non-firing transports.** `BomberEncounter` (mission 1) already flies a required non-firing group with escorts; reuse it with count 3, the measured speed and a route (world spec §2). Escape-as-failure is an open question (§11).
4. **Escort reinforcements beyond the listed count**, in three classes, plus an optional named ace mid-fight (mission 1 has `aceName`/`aceAt`).
5. **Per-target points** 160/140/60/240 and rank thresholds on the points basis: supported by `scoring.basis: "points"`, which needs new roles (transport, jammer).
6. **12:00 countdown**: supported (`countdownSeconds`).
7. **High-altitude fight over land.** ALT 4,000–5,800 ft is 1,170–1,700 studs at 0.96 studs per metre (+32 sea level), inside `FlightMath.MaxAltitude` 3,600; the world needs forested hills well below (world spec).
8. **Weapons.** The reference player used only guns and standard missiles. SKYBREAK's guided missiles and specials (UAV, flares, salvo, lance) cover this; the reference's long-range ground missile special has no use here.
9. **Large aircraft models:** a four-engine transport and a twin-engine radar jammer (world spec §6; each new Blender asset needs the upload safety gate).

## 2. Summary

A single-phase air intercept over open sea and a large forested island, flown in early-afternoon overcast. After a cinematic carrier launch the player starts airborne about 2,000 ft up, heading north, with a flight of friendly fighters. AWACS gives the only task: catch six heavy bombers flying north over the island and shoot all of them down. The bombers do not fight back. They fly a fixed route over a coastal city and then a friendly airbase, where at least one drops its bombs. MiG-21 and F-5E-class escort fighters mix with them and lock onto the player. They are optional, but they score points. A higher-value named escort ace appears about 2.5 minutes in. The bombers are faster than the player's cruise speed, so the footage player flew on afterburner most of the time (HUD speed 1,200–1,650 against about 600 at cruise). The mission ends the moment the last bomber dies. There is no return to base and no landing. A 10:00 countdown runs on the HUD. The footage player finished with 6:08.81 left, scoring 960 points and rank S.

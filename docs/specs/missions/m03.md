## 2. Summary

A two-stage mission in snow-covered mountains under a clear sky. The player starts airborne about 1,290 ft up, heading north-west, with three friendly fighters. AWACS warns that this is enemy airspace, gives the home bearing in advance (south) and says a safe return completes the job. **Nine radar domes at two sites are the required targets**: five on a long ridge crest about 2 minutes north-west of the start, and four on a high saddle about 13 km further west-north-west. Each site has AA guns (four in all, optional, 20 points), and the second has a parked transport helicopter (optional, 40). Light fighters defend from the start; a second flight of three, including a named ace, arrives right after the first site falls. When the last dome dies (5:35 in), a MISSION UPDATE resets the clock and AWACS orders everyone south across a **return line** shown on the map. Crossing it (1:12 later) cuts to an automatic cockpit approach and landing at the home airbase, which the player can skip. MISSION ACCOMPLISHED appears on the runway. The footage player took no damage and scored **1,680 points, rank S**, in 6:46.71 of mission time.

## 10. Engine gaps (today's SKYBREAK)

## 2. Summary

A sustained daytime attack through three connected harbour basins. Warships, support ships and dockside industry form a dense target field, with fighters overhead and weapon components on ships. The player can return to the land runway to rearm, then resumes the strike. Reaching the points goal does not end the mission: the sortie runs to clock expiry.

Observed final result: **6,260 points / rank S** (1:20:19). This is a demonstrated S result, **not an exact S cutoff**. See §7 before choosing rank cuts.

A large daylight fighter battle above a green island and surrounding blue sea. The initial seven-minute points window continues even after the score goal is met. At expiry a mission update reveals bombers approaching from the west and resets the countdown to six minutes. The launch site stays visible below the fight; a rocket rises before completion. For SKYBREAK the launch payoff becomes the loyalist broadcast, as approved in the chapter map.

## 2. Summary

A long clear-day attack across forested mountain valleys, airfields and river docks. Score at least 3,000 before the 20-minute strike clock expires; keep attacking for rank after the minimum. Repeated distant artillery warnings force the player below the ridge line. A mid-sortie return, landing and takeoff preserves the strike. The player scores 6,190/S; this is a demonstrated S result, not the exact S threshold.

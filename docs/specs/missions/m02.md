## 2. Summary

A single-objective ground strike on an enemy-held airbase set in farmland, under a flat overcast sky in early afternoon. The player starts airborne about 1,600 ft up, heading north, with three or more friendly fighters. AWACS points the flight along a power line that leads north to the base, about 2 minutes away, and orders it to destroy the heavy bombers parked there before they can raid. On the way, a substation under the power line is the first thing the player hits (optional, 100 points). Light fighters meet the flight about a minute in; more scramble from the base's own runway while the player attacks. The base defends itself with anti-aircraft guns, and the player took real damage (4% from the ground, then 65% from a missile hit). The **11 parked bombers** are the only required targets. Hangars, AA guns, the control tower and the substation are optional point targets, and the footage player destroyed every ground object on the base. A named ace in a ground-attack jet joins the defence. The mission ends the moment the last bomber dies. Then a friendly flight radios that it is heading home: no landing. A 15:00 countdown runs on the HUD. The footage player finished in **6:29.08**, scoring **2,610 points, rank S**.

## 10. Engine gaps (today's SKYBREAK)

From reading `src/shared/missions/m01.ts`, `MissionCatalog.ts`, `MissionPolicy.ts`, `WeaponRules.ts`, `SpecialWeapons.ts`, `UploadedAssetSpecs.ts` and `server/PhaseDirector.ts` at `main` 07526a2. Engineers should confirm.

1. **Bombs.** The reference uses unguided napalm (6 bombs, area fire that kills groups of 2–3 parked aircraft per drop).

## 2. Summary

A pre-dawn strike on an enemy petrochemical complex that fuels the enemy fleet. It has two parts: an offshore oil-rig complex out at sea and a land refinery on the coast, both defended by AA guns, SAMs, interceptors and helicopters, with tankers at sea. The player starts airborne at about 1,750 ft heading north with two allied flights. The objective is a **score threshold**: at least **2,000 points within the 10:00 time limit**, and keep destroying targets until the time runs out. The footage player hit tankers and the rig first, passed 2,000 at T+4:03, flew back to the friendly base, landed at night, rearmed and took off, then hit the land refinery until the clock ran out. As phase 1 ends, a **surprise elite squadron of five fighters** arrives. AWACS orders everyone south and a new 4:00 clock starts. The player withdrew south across the boundary line without killing any of them, and the mission ended at 1:47.35 into that clock. The footage player scored 4,860 points (85 ground targets, 2 ships, 5 aircraft), rank S.

## 10. Engine gaps (today's SKYBREAK)

From `MissionCatalog.ts`, `MissionPolicy.ts`, `WeaponRules.ts`, `SpecialWeapons.ts`, `EnvironmentPresets.ts` and `WorldPlan.ts` at `main` 07526a2. Engineers should confirm.

1. **Score-threshold objective.** There is no phase kind "reach N points by the clock". Needed: a timed phase that succeeds at clock expiry if PTS ≥ goal (and fails otherwise), with HUD `PTS/2000` until the goal is met.
2. **Large ground-target fields.** 88 ground targets of about 11 types plus 2 ships. Today `GroundAsset` has four kinds and the ground role scores a flat 150. Needed: per-type point values (20–120), many cheap targets (AA guns, tanks) and the radar's ground clutter.
3. **Mid-mission RTB, landing and rearm.** Not present. Needed: an RTB trigger (cross the line or a prompt), an assisted or skippable landing at the friendly base with landing and takeoff clocks, a pause of the mission clock, a refill of missiles and special, and a TAKEOFF / CHANGE WEAPON choice. A simpler first version could be a skippable cinematic plus refill.
4. **Surprise phase on clock expiry.** `PhaseTwist` inserts phases on progress, but this needs a phase-end MISSION UPDATE, a 4:00 phase clock, a HUD-less cut-in with the clock paused, and success on withdrawing south across the boundary line.
5. **Elite squadron AI:** five top-tier fighters that prefer allies, then the player; optional 150-point kills. Today's `ace` is a single required target.
6. **Night lighting.** `ocean_night`, `relay_night` and `city_night` exist, but nothing gives a lit industrial complex: point-light fields, searchlight beams that sweep, glare and a moon.
7. **Bombs.** The reference player used unguided bombs for most ground kills. SKYBREAK has guided missiles (ground lock out to 2,450 studs, 80° cone), the 4AAM salvo and the XLAA lance (ground lock 3,800). These substitute; an unguided-bomb special is optional future work, not required.
8. **Naval targets** (tankers) at sea: not a target kind today.
9. **Helicopters** as air targets.
10. **Two allied flights** with radio, one of which can be lost.

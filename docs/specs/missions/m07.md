## 2. Summary

A northbound dusk penetration, following a trail of reflective solar arrays into a power plant. Fighters contest the approach; helicopters and AA defend the array field. Destroying the indicated installations produces a mission update: distant artillery is firing into the area. The player descends into a winding rock canyon and exits south. The final two minutes are survival and terrain masking, not another target quota.

Observed final result: **2,245 points / rank S** (1:36:02). This is a demonstrated S result, **not an exact S cutoff**. See §7 before choosing rank cuts.

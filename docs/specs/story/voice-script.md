## Generation handoff

After building and running the offline relay extraction, `bun tools/voice-lines.ts` refreshes `assets/voice/lines.json` (the generation input). `python3 tools/voice.py plan` lists reused takes, new banks and the lines that need generation, with credits, without contacting ElevenLabs. The orchestrator supplies a key file securely for `voice.py generate --key-file … --budget … --only …`. Casting and spoken replacements live in `assets/voice/casting.json`. The cache hashes voice/model/settings/text, so only edited lines regenerate; a take found in an approved live bank (same cache key, request id and raw sha256) is never generated or uploaded again.

`voice.py pack` packs each complete tier and rewrites VoiceCues and transcripts. Then `voice.py stt`, `voice.py checklist` (heard-text lexicon, non-speech/stray-word gate), an independent listening review per bank, `tools/upload-voice.py --only <bank>`, moderation and permissions are separate steps. Do not overwrite approved cues with invented transcripts. New text stays silent until its exact cue and approved bank exist. After repacking, regenerate this manifest and update its inventory counts.

## Method (common to all four assets)

- **Integrity:** I recomputed SHA-256 from the file bytes for every file in each folder. I compared the hashes against each checklist's `sha256` map in both directions and confirmed that the `previews` list matches the `preview-*.png` files present. I rechecked the FBX hashes inside Blender at import time.
- **Embedded textures:** first, a byte scan of each FBX for PNG signature-to-`IEND` streams found exactly four streams per FBX. Second, I imported each FBX in Blender with image search disabled and hashed each packed image's bytes. Every stream and packed image matches its external map exactly, so neither path depended on the external files.
- **Independent renders:** I used `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup`, Blender **5.2.1 LTS**, with my scripts outside the asset folders (`/tmp/pr152-tools/render.py`, `tile.py`). Each exact FBX was imported, and its embedded maps were connected to a Principled PBR material: base colour (sRGB), and metallic, roughness and tangent-space normal (non-colour). Imported geometry and UVs were preserved. Rendering used Cycles at 768 × 768, 24 samples, denoised.
- **Viewpoints (21 per lighting setup):** orthographic views along ±X, ±Y, top and bottom; eight perspective three-quarter views (four high, four low, one from each diagonal); and seven closeups (one at each of the four sides, one at the top, and two front three-quarter views).
- **Lighting setups:** **neutral** used a mid-grey world with three white suns (key, fill and underside). **Contrasting** used a dark world with a warm key and a cool opposing fill.
- **Inspection:** I inspected every render in six-tile sheets, each tile shown at roughly 660 px. I also inspected four 4× nearest-neighbour crops of each colour map's quadrants for small text or emblems.
- **Limits:** the renders use a reconstructed shader and my own lighting, not Roblox's renderer. No Studio import, upload or Roblox Player test was performed. No exhaustive third-party IP clearance is claimed. The local evidence (renders, sheets, `technical.json`, `manifest.json`) is under `/tmp/pr152-tools/`. It is temporary review evidence, not committed repository content.

### Findings

**Face/creature scrutiny (the creator's flagged concern).** Before writing this section, I looked specifically for paired eyes, a mouth, a mask, goggles, a skull, an owl or an insect face from every angle. I found none.

- **Front panel:** the layout is clearly asymmetric. The top row has a long dark-teal window on the model's right and one small cream meter with a single needle on its left. The bottom row has a wide slotted grille under the window, one large tuning knob, a column of two small knobs and a column of three toggles at the far edge. No feature is mirrored across the centre line, and no central feature sits under a pair. In my orthographic front views (`negY`, both lights) and the front closeups, it reads as a radio receiver, not a face.
- **Top deck (highest-risk view, since two round reels could read as eyes):** the two reels are visibly different: one has a nearly full brown tape pack and the other a nearly empty one. They are staggered front to back (checklist hubs +0.27 and −0.13 studs), each has three open spokes, and a visible brown tape path joins them through a head block, capstan and pinch roller. The five transport keys sit in one corner, not under the reels as a "mouth". In `top-*`, `closeup-top-*` and both high three-quarter views, the composition reads unambiguously as a reel-to-reel tape transport. I judge this reading strong enough that no reasonable viewer would see a face, including under the warm/cool contrast lighting.
- **Rear:** seven vertical heat-sink fins and two tiny round connectors at the lower corner. The connectors are small and off-centre, with nothing beneath them, so there is no face reading.
- **Sides:** one carry handle each. A plain slab with a protruding knob/toggle edge.
- **Bottom:** a plain mottled panel with four rubber feet.

**Text, symbols and IP.** The window is a plain pane. The meter has no scale or numerals, and the keys are plain coloured blocks. In the four 4× colour-map crops, I found only UV-island fills, ring islands for knobs/reels, and baked slot and dot patterns. There are no letters, numerals, logos, badges, flags, insignia or trademarks. The design is a generic field-radio/tape-deck form, not a copy of an identifiable branded product.

**Content categories.** There is no human figure, skin texture, nudity, or sexual or body-like reading. There is no gore, weapon or damage glorification, no hate, extremist, political or gang symbol, no gesture, no real-world military insignia, and no identifiable trademark.

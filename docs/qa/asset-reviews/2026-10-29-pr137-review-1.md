## Checklist results

All four approved assets passed all seven checks:
1. No text, numbers, logos, hull or tail numbers (maps and assembled views).
2. No insignia, flags, emblems or extremist symbols.
3. No people, skin or faces.
4. Nothing suggestive, gory or excessively violent.
5. Original design.
6. Technical sanity.
7. Complete record with no remaining doubt.

Notes per asset:
- **Heavy transport.** The pale multi-pane glazing reads as a grid. The body-colour nose and four separate engine pods form no eye-and-mouth arrangement in any frontal view. 8,492 of 15,000 triangles; 1024² maps; 72.68 × 19.60 × 67.55 studs.
- **Radar jammer.** The creator flagged the dark fan faces in frontal views. They sit on separate wing nacelles below and outside the fuselage, and do not combine with the panes or nose into a face. The rotodome circles read as structural ribs. 6,612 triangles; 1024² maps; 59.32 × 15.88 × 62.18 studs.
- **Fuel tanker.** No name, hull number or draught marks. The short top-view mast bar reads as physical structure. The bow has no paired dark marks. 2,682 of 6,000 triangles; 512² maps; 46.0 × 59.6 × 266.68 studs. The orientation aid shows the bow toward −Z, with the crane, catwalk and rescue boat on starboard (+X), and the waterline offset at 16 studs. The imported heading and waterline still need checking during integration; this is a correctness item.
- **Attack helicopter.** The opaque canopy shows no crew. The single broad engine fairing avoids paired frontal openings, and the sideways exhausts form no rear-facing eyes. Parts are `Body`, `MainRotor` and `TailRotor`; 3,436 triangles; 1024² maps. The tail rotor is on port (−X), with the nose toward −Z. The imported tail rotor side and the rotor pivots still need checking during integration.

**Vanta v2 elite, rejected on check 3 and therefore check 7.** In `preview-back.png`, and the same view on the contact sheet, two prominent dark circular exhaust openings flank a smaller central opening above a curved lower silhouette. Together these permit an owl- or mask-like face reading. An earlier approval of the shared airframe does not resolve this review's independent concern. This finding applies the project's stricter "anything that reads as a face" rule; it does not say Roblox prohibits twin-exhaust aircraft. The fin and rudder gap is not the reason for rejection. Required: revise the rear geometry, contrast or opening arrangement; produce a new versioned export; obtain a new independent review. Do not upload `vanta-v2-elite-1`.

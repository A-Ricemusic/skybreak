## Findings

### 1. Revision 2 changes are present, but the rear face/mask concern remains unresolved

The central dark tail-boom opening is gone. The nozzle exits are capped with lighter metal discs and radial vanes. The broad aft panel is lighter and no longer has the previous dark soot treatment.

However, **`preview-back.png` and `preview-closeup-rear.png` still permit a clear goggles/mask reading**:

- Two similarly sized circular nozzle discs remain symmetrically placed at eye spacing.
- The broad panel connects and surrounds both circles like a mask.
- Its lower edge rises centrally into a nose-bridge shape and falls toward both sides.
- The paired lower fins reinforce the enclosing silhouette.

The independent `independent-rear.png` and especially `independent-rear-closeup.png` reproduce this reading from the exact exported FBX, with its embedded textures and UVs applied. It therefore cannot be dismissed as a creator-preview artifact. Lighter plugs remove the dark-hole/pupil feature but do not remove the larger paired-eye-and-mask composition.

Rear oblique, high and low views were inspected; they show the revised mechanical construction more clearly. They do not resolve the straight-rear concern.

This finding applies the stricter project face-reading criterion established in review-1. It is **not** a finding that Roblox prohibits twin-exhaust aircraft or ordinary mechanical discs.

### 2. Radial vane and symbol assessment

Each nozzle has ten straight radial vanes around a plain centre. The visible assembly reads as a mechanical spoked disc. I found no hooked arms, lightning-bolt rays, runic centre, recognizable extremist emblem or accompanying ideological markings.

The inspected arrangement does not match the specific Black Sun or swastika structures described in the consulted references. A generic wheel/sun resemblance is not by itself evidence of extremist content. Conversely, choosing ten vanes instead of eight, twelve or twenty-four is not a universal safety guarantee; the arrangement and context must still be reviewed.

**No separate extremist-symbol rejection is raised.** The blocking finding is the assembled rear mask/face reading.

### 3. Other inspected content

No readable text, numerals, logos, real-world military insignia, flags, identifiable trademark, skull emblem or gesture was found on the inspected asset surfaces or maps. The fin/rudder gap reads as a mechanical seam in a pale field; it is not the reason for rejection.

No skin texture, human figure, nudity, sexual anatomy, suggestive body depiction, gore or victim depiction was found. The canopy views show no occupant. The pylons carry ordinary unmarked missile forms; no kill marks, real-world weapon construction instructions or extremist glorification were found.

The texture maps show material fields, camouflage and mechanical surface detail. No hidden text or emblem was identified in the reviewed maps or assembled views.

## Required fixes

1. **Break the paired-circle-and-mask rear composition geometrically.** A concrete candidate is non-circular flattened exhaust exits with plain interiors, combined with an aft panel whose lower silhouette does not form a central nose bridge around two circular features. An alternative is a single continuous exhaust opening. These are design candidates requiring review, not pre-approved solutions.
2. Do not rely solely on making the circular centres brighter, changing vane count or removing the central boom; revision 2 demonstrates that those changes leave the larger mask reading.
3. Produce a newly versioned export, rebake all affected maps and regenerate the complete preview pack and hashes.
4. Include straight rear, rear close-up, rear-low, rear-high and both rear three-quarter views under neutral and contrasting lighting. Obtain a new independent review of the exact export before upload.

## Findings

### 1. The rear face/mask concern from reviews 1 and 2 is resolved

The Revision 3 claims are visually confirmed in the creator previews and in my own renders of the exact FBX:

- **One continuous rectangular exhaust:** the two round nozzle assemblies are gone. The tail ends in a single wide, short rectangular fairing. It has dark upper and lower lips and one undivided, plain mid-grey exit plate. There are no circles, vanes, divider, dark holes or paired features.
- **Flattened aft belly centre:** in the straight rear and rear-low views, the lower aft silhouette no longer rises centrally into a nose-bridge shape.
- **Ventral strakes removed:** no paired lower fins appear in the rear, rear-low, bottom or side views.

I looked specifically for the reading that caused the earlier rejections: paired eyes at eye spacing, an enclosing mask panel, goggles, a nose bridge, or an owl or skull composition. I looked in:

- `preview-back`, `preview-closeup-rear`, `preview-back-low`, `preview-back-high`, both rear three-quarter views and `preview-closeup-rear-3q`, each under both lighting setups;
- my own `rear`, `rear-closeup`, `rear-closeup-low`, `rear-low`, `rear-high`, `rear-3q-left` and `rear-3q-right` renders, under both lighting setups.

No paired-eye or mask composition is present. The single rectangle reads as a mechanical two-dimensional thrust-vectoring nozzle on a boxy fairing. With no paired features, no central division and no mouth-and-eye arrangement, I find no face reading even under the project's stricter criterion.

### 2. Other viewing angles

- **Front and front-low:** two rectangular side intakes, a nose cone and a canopy. This is the ordinary twin-intake fighter arrangement and the same forward airframe as the default Vanta. It has no added eye markings, pupils, teeth or mouth livery. Under the brief's instruction not to reject concerns that would apply to any ordinary jet, it is not a finding.
- **Front close-up:** the canopy shows no occupant. The pitot and sensors are plain mechanical items.
- **Top and bottom:** the planform is an ordinary delta with canards. The camouflage polygons, wing bands and pale fin are abstract. No symbol, letterform, cross, swastika-like, sun-wheel or runic arrangement is formed from any angle.
- **Sides and three-quarters:** an ordinary fighter profile. The small pale olive strips on the nose sides and the fin tip are plain formation-light or antenna-style patches, not text.

### 3. Texture maps

The colour, metalness, roughness and normal maps contain UV-island material fills, camouflage patches, panel lines and mechanical surface detail. In the 2× quadrant crops of the colour map, I found no readable text, numerals, logos, flags, real-world military roundels or insignia, trademark, skull, face or emblem. A few tiny radial-line discs on small mechanical islands, such as missile or pod end caps, are sub-centimetre at game scale and do not form any symbol.

### 4. Content categories

- No skin texture, human figure, nudity, sexual or body-like reading, gore or victim depiction.
- No hate or extremist symbol, gesture, political symbol or real-world sensitive-event reference.
- No real-world military insignia or identifiable trademark. The airframe is a fictional design, and a rectangular thrust-vectoring nozzle is a generic engineering form.
- The pylons carry ordinary unmarked missile shapes, consistent with game violence that the Community Standards permit; no kill marks or glorification.

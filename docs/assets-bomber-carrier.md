# Mission 1 assets: heavy bomber and aircraft carrier

Two new Blender upload assets for mission 1 (`docs/specs/missions/m01.md`,
`docs/specs/worlds/m01.md`). Both are generated by `tools/assets/m01_assets.py`
through the normal [upload pipeline](roblox-asset-pipeline.md).

**Status: exported, not uploaded, not wired into the game.** Each folder needs an
independent safety review under the AGENTS.md "Blender asset safety review" gate
(10/10 verdict) before anything is uploaded. The folders are deliberately left
out of `write_manifest.py`'s `KEYS`, so `manifest.json`,
`src/shared/UploadedAssetSpecs.ts` and `src/shared/RobloxAssetIds.ts` do not
mention them and nothing in the live game can load them.

## Regenerate

```sh
tools/assets/build-all.sh heavy-bomber carrier       # both, then write_manifest.py (unchanged output)
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
    --python tools/assets/build.py -- heavy-bomber   # one asset
SKYASSET_DRAFT=1 /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
    --python tools/assets/build.py -- carrier        # geometry-only Workbench draft in build/draft/
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
    --python tools/assets/verify_fbx.py              # FBX round trip for every folder
```

Each build runs the standard `Asset.finish()` (Cycles PBR bake, four previews, FBX
with embedded textures, checklist). Then `review_pack()` adds:

- review renders with the final baked material: back, port side and bottom
  (orthographic), four more 3/4 views (front-right, rear-left, rear-right, low front)
  and close-ups, with a key, fill and under-light and no ground plane;
- `preview-scale-reference.png`: an approved, already uploaded asset (Vanta v2 beside
  the bomber, Kestrel v2 on the carrier's catapult) imported at in-game size after
  the FBX is written. It is a review aid only and is never part of the export;
- `contact-sheet.png` with every preview on one image (`contact_sheet.py`);
- SHA-256 hashes of the FBX and the four textures in `checklist.json` (`sha256`);
- the same checks `write_manifest.py` applies (all moderation booleans true,
  triangle and texture budgets, every listed preview present). A failure stops the build.

A rebuild changes only the FBX's embedded timestamp and the Eevee noise (as for
every asset), so the FBX hash changes on every rebuild. The reviewer must approve
the hashes recorded in the committed checklist, and the uploader must upload that
exact file.

## Heavy bomber (`assets/roblox-upload/heavy-bomber/`)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Size (studs, X × Y × Z) | 72.74 × 16.6 × 64.8 (span × height × length) |
| Triangles | 9,364 (aircraft limit 15,000) |
| Parts | 1 (`Body`); no control surfaces |
| Textures | 4 × 1024² (colour, normal, roughness, metalness) |
| Pivot | bounds centre, -Z forward; `fbxMetresPerStud` 0.01 (imports at authored size) |
| Extra checklist data | `attachments` (wingtips, mid-engine exhaust), `engineExhausts` (four nozzle points) |

Design (original; D-001): a long, slender fuselage with an upswept tail and a
faired cockpit hump; a shoulder-mounted wing with 33° leading-edge sweep and 4°
anhedral passing over a wing-box fairing; four podded turbofans on pylons; a
swept fin over low-set tailplanes with dihedral; big landing-gear sponsons,
flap-track fairings, wing fences and wingtip pods; a refuelling probe offset to
starboard. It is unarmed: no turret, guns, missiles or visible bombs, and the
bomb-bay doors are closed panel lines. The deliberate differences from the
reference footage's four-turboprop bomber are turbofans instead of contra-rotating
propellers, a shoulder wing with anhedral and podded engines.

Paint follows the Vanta enemy family: upper `#4a5159` and lower `#6c757e`
countershading with the Vanta's dark splinter camouflage, scaled for the larger
airframe. The radome is only a shade darker than the body, the windscreen is one
continuous framed dark band, and there are panel lines, a bomb-bay outline,
streaks and engine soot. The only markings are plain dark-red bands (`#6e2a24`,
the Vanta accent) on the fin tip and engine lips. There is no insignia, number or
text.

Size: 72.7 studs of span is 2.96× the Vanta v2 (24.6) and 3.28× the Kestrel v2
(22.2). The game's fighters are 26 studs long for real fighters of about 18 m,
about 1.44 studs per metre, so a heavy bomber of the ~50 m span class comes out
at 72 studs. That keeps it at the same scale as the fighters. It reads
unmistakably large next to the escorts, and it is still compact enough for six in
formation inside the ±3,600-stud airspace.

## Aircraft carrier (`assets/roblox-upload/carrier/`)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Size (studs, X × Y × Z) | 181.0 × 153.8 × 560.0 (width incl. sponsons and masts × height × length) |
| Triangles | 2,772 (large-prop limit 6,000) |
| Parts | 1 (`carrier`) |
| Textures | 4 × 512² |
| Pivot | bottom centre; `groundOffset` 20 (waterline above the keel); flight deck top 54 studs above the keel |
| Extra checklist data | `catapults` (start and end of both tracks, Roblox model space), `deckHeightStuds`, `launchHeading` -Z |

Design (original; D-001): a waterline hull with a raked, flared bow and a transom
stern. A gallery deck sits under an overhanging flight deck with a 9° angled
landing area to port. The island is aft of midships on the starboard side (Blender
+X, the pipeline's "right"): stepped sloped blocks, a bridge window band, an
enclosed mast with four flat panel faces, two radomes and an exhaust stack. There
are two bow catapults with flush blast-deflector plates and shuttles, three
deck-edge elevators (two starboard, one port), hangar-bay doors under the
overhang, catwalks with life-raft canisters, lowered deck-edge masts, four
sponsons with covered point-defence domes, and knee braces under the stern
overhang. The paint is a haze-grey hull, a black boot-top, red antifouling below
the waterline and a dark non-skid deck with tyre smudges.

Deck markings are straight lines only: landing-area edges, a dashed centre line
that starts ahead of the wires, three arresting-wire lines, catapult track lines
with a dark slot, elevator outlines, a starboard parking line and a dashed foul
line. No two lines cross. There is no hull number, name, circle, cross or other
glyph-like mark. No parked aircraft are baked in: a Kestrel mesh would blow the
6,000-triangle budget, and the game can place Kestrel models on deck. No deck crew
is modelled.

Size: 560 studs is 21.5 Kestrel lengths. Real big-deck carriers are about 18–21
lengths of their fighters. The deck's 141-stud width is 6.3 Kestrel spans (real
about 5.6–6.4), and each catapult track is about 155 studs, 6 Kestrel lengths. A
26-stud fighter on the bow therefore looks right (see
`preview-scale-reference.png`). Matching the fighter scale exactly would give
about 470 studs, which would make the carrier barely longer than the existing
430-stud frigate. That frigate is authored at real-world 0.28 m per stud, so next
to this carrier it is still oversized (0.77 of its length, against about 0.4 for a
real escort). The intro should keep escort frigates well away from the carrier or
scale them down with `Model:ScaleTo`.

Use as the mission start: `catapults[i].from` and `.to` in the checklist are model-space
points (bottom-centre pivot, -Z forward) on the deck surface. A scripted fly-off can
run a Kestrel along either track and off the bow.

## Concerns for the safety reviewer and the uploader

- Orientation: the island is authored on Blender +X. The pipeline treats that as
  the model's right (starboard), but no asymmetric prop has yet been checked
  in-game for which side it lands on after the Roblox FBX import. Every prop so far
  is mirror-symmetric. If the island appears on the port side, the asset is still
  an original design. Mirroring needs a re-export and a new review.
- The 512² carrier maps give about one texel per stud, so deck lines are 2–3 texels
  wide and soft up close (same limit as the frigate). 1024² would sharpen them at
  4× the texture memory; that budget change was not made here.
- The bomber's camouflage pattern is projected in world space, so it continues
  across the nacelles and pylons (visible in `preview-closeup-engines.png`).

## Wiring after approval (not done here)

1. Upload `heavy-bomber.fbx` and `carrier.fbx` (the approved hashes) as Model assets.
2. Add `"heavy-bomber": "HeavyBomber"` and `"carrier": "Carrier"` to
   `write_manifest.KEYS`. The bomber is authored at game size, so leave it out of
   `PROCEDURAL` and it keeps gameScale 1. Rerun `write_manifest.py`, then add both
   IDs to `RobloxAssetIds.ts` (the type requires every key).
3. Use the bomber through `UploadedAssets` and the carrier through `WorldAssets.PlaceUploaded`.
